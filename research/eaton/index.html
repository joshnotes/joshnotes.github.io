Long Eaton: The market is pricing a slowdown the order book hasn't shown

Record orders, a third guidance raise, and a stock down 23% in a month. The multiple now sits below its five-year average on numbers that are still going up.

Summary

  • Eaton reported record third-quarter results on November 3 and raised guidance for the third time this year. The stock fell 9.9% the next day and 23% over the month to November 6.
  • Demand indicators point the other way. Rolling 12-month organic orders rose 39% in Electrical Americas and 51% in Electrical Global, and Electrical backlog grew 46%.
  • At about 22x forward earnings against a five-year average of 25x, the shares price in a slowdown that none of Eaton's reported numbers show yet. The target applies 25x to 2027 consensus EPS of $16.28.

The business

Eaton makes the equipment that moves electricity safely from the grid to the load: switchgear, power distribution, circuit protection, UPS systems and, since closing the $9.55 billion Boyd Thermal acquisition in March, liquid cooling for data centers. Smaller Aerospace and Mobility segments round it out.

Electrical Americas is the engine: about 47% of sales and the highest margin in the company. Its data-center sales grew 54% in the quarter. Total sales rose 28%, of which 19 points were organic, 8 came from acquisitions and 1 from currency. Adjusted EPS of $3.68 beat the $3.54 consensus.

Thesis 1: Demand is still accelerating

In this business, orders lead revenue by quarters, sometimes years. Order dollars hit records in all three of the segments that report them, and total Electrical backlog topped $20 billion. Management raised 2026 organic growth guidance to 13–15% and adjusted EPS guidance to $13.70–13.80, above the $13.67 consensus going in.

Thesis 2: The constraint is capacity, and Eaton is adding it

The largest buyers of AI infrastructure are being slowed by physical capacity, not demand: Microsoft trimmed its 2026 capex forecast because contracted third-party capacity arrived late. That is deferred demand for exactly what Eaton sells. Eaton is spending to meet it, with $275 million of capex in the quarter and a €810 million (about $921 million) agreement to buy COL Group, an Italian maker of medium-voltage distribution equipment, to add European capacity.

Thesis 3: The multiple has already reset

The stock traded near 33x forward earnings at its April peak. It now trades near 22x, below its five-year average of 25x. The selloff came from outside the company: worries about Meta's capex and free cash flow, the midterm results and a Texas pledge to pause data-center approvals, and an AI-led market rout on November 5. None of those changed Eaton's reported orders.

The bear case assumes data-center orders roll over in 2027, EPS grows only about 5% from the 2026 guidance midpoint, and the multiple falls to 18x. The bull case assumes backlog conversion beats consensus by about 4% and the market pays 28x, still below April's peak. The Street's mean target was $484 as of November 4; this target is deliberately below it.

Risks

  • Permitting and politics. Local pauses on new data centers could show up in 2027 orders.
  • Hyperscaler funding. If free cash flow pressure forces capex cuts, Eaton's orders follow with a lag.
  • Rates. With the 10-year Treasury above 5%, multiples across the market are under pressure, and 25x may prove too generous.
  • Execution. About $11 billion of acquisitions this year (Boyd Thermal and Ultra PCS) plus fast capacity additions raise integration risk.
  • Cyclical end markets. Outside data centers, industrial and commercial demand would soften in a slowdown.

Catalysts

  • Fourth-quarter results in February, where rolling orders and book-to-bill matter most.
  • Microsoft's delayed third-party capacity coming online in the second half of its fiscal year.
  • The COL Group acquisition, expected to close in the first quarter of 2027.

What would change the view

Rolling 12-month orders in Electrical Americas turning negative, or book-to-bill falling below 1. Either would mean the slowdown the market is pricing has started.

Prices as of the November 6, 2026 close; the shares have been volatile since. Personal research based on public information, not investment advice.
