Notes

The AI story didn't break this week. Trust in it did.

The 10-year Treasury yield fell this week, and the S&P 500 dropped 8.6% anyway. Investors are charging more to hold risk, and the AI trade sits in the middle of it.

Did Microsoft just tell us where the AI bottleneck is?

Microsoft is spending as fast as it can and still running short of AI capacity. The holdup is buildings, grid connections and the electrical gear in between.

Private credit's defaults are hiding in IOUs, not courtrooms

Fitch's default rate has been at or above 6% since April. Many of those borrowers now pay interest with more debt instead of cash, which delays the losses without avoiding them.

Wall Street bonuses are up again. 2026 might be the peak.

Johnson Associates expects bonuses to rise for a third straight year, led by equity traders and underwriters. The harder question is whether the deals that paid for them keep coming at today's rates.
