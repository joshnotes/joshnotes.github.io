How I work.

I started working at 14, behind the counter of my dad's store. Everything since, from a Wendy's register to an Amazon warehouse to five finance internships, taught me the same lesson: show the work.

I didn't come up through a target school or a family network, so I'd rather let the work speak. When I look at a company, I go end to end: how the business makes money, what the market might be missing, a model I build myself, and the risks that would prove me wrong.

I learn best by teaching. Explaining something to people who will check my numbers keeps the work honest, and it is the fastest way I know to understand an industry.
